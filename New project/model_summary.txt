RealValue AI is an explainable property valuation engine for the Chennai real estate market powered by an XGBoost Regressor (500 trees, depth 5). Evaluated on strict chronological splits (70% train, 15% calibration, 15% test) to prevent future data leakage, it achieves a 1.22% Mean Absolute Percentage Error with an MAE of ₹1.51 Lakhs. Beyond point predictions, it delivers distribution-free uncertainty quantification through a 90% split-conformal interval (±₹3.17 Lakhs radius). Valuations are made transparent using TreeSHAP local feature attributions and validated with automated safety guardrails that cross-reference locality price-per-square-foot benchmarks and retrieve nearest historical sales to flag anomalies for human review.
